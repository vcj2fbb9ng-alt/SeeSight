How it works

Three steps from receipt to decision.

1. Add a receipt

Take a photo or upload a file. SeeSight reads it and creates the expense record.

2. Check and send

Confirm the details, add the purpose of the trip and send the claim to your assigned accountant.

3. Get a decision

Your accountant decides with one comment. You get an email, and a PDF with the receipt image.

The full flow
Features

Everything the process needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Receipt reading

Photo or file in, expense record out: merchant, date and amount filled in.

Early warnings

Over-limit and duplicate expenses are flagged before the claim is sent.

Always-on status

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is SeeSight?

A web platform for business travel expenses. Travelers add receipts, SeeSight turns them into expense records, and one assigned accountant reviews and decides.

Do travelers need to install an app?

No. SeeSight works in the phone browser.

Who reviews a claim?

Each traveler is assigned to one accountant, who approves, rejects, approves part of the claim or asks for a document, with one comment.

What happens after a decision?

The traveler gets an email. The claim can be exported as a PDF that includes the receipt image.
