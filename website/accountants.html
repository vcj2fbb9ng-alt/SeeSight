FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do rules work?

You set a rule once, for example "Hotel: up to 250 GEL per night". Any expense above it is flagged for you.

What if more than one rule matches?

The first matching rule wins. You can reorder rules at any time.

Which claims do I see?

Claims from the travelers assigned to you. Each traveler has one assigned accountant.

What can I do with a claim?

Approve it, reject it, approve part of it or ask for a document, with one comment. The traveler is notified by email.

Can I get a PDF for our records?

Yes. The PDF export includes the receipt image.
