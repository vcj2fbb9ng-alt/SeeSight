Claim status

Four states. Zero guessing.

  1. Draft

    You're adding receipts and the trip purpose.

  2. Submitted

    Sent to your assigned accountant.

  3. In review

    Your accountant is looking at it.

  4. Decision

    You get an email with the outcome and a comment.

Step by step

From the taxi to approved, in five steps.

  1. Add a receipt

    Take a photo or upload a file from your phone browser.

  2. Check the expense

    SeeSight reads the merchant, date and amount. You confirm it.

  3. Add the trip purpose

    One line on why you travelled, so your accountant has the context.

  4. Send it

    The claim goes to your one assigned accountant.

  5. Get the answer

    You get an email with the decision, plus a PDF with the receipt image.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to download an app?

No. SeeSight works in the browser on your phone.

What if I lost a receipt?

Lost receipts are handled in the same flow, so you can still add the expense and send the claim.

How do per diems work?

Per diem is handled in the same flow as your other expenses.

Who looks at my claim?

Your one assigned accountant. They can approve it, reject it, approve part of it or ask for a document, with one comment.

How do I know if something is wrong before I send?

SeeSight flags expenses that are over a limit or look like duplicates before you send the claim.
