How it works

From receipt to decision in four steps.

One simple flow connects the traveler and their accountant, with the company seeing everything.

  1. 1Traveler

    Add a receipt

    A photo or a file. SeeSight reads it and creates the expense.

  2. 2Traveler

    Check and send

    Confirm the details, add the trip purpose and send it to the accountant.

  3. 3Accountant

    Decide

    Approve, reject, approve part or ask for a document, with one comment.

  4. 4Everyone

    Email and PDF

    The traveler gets an email. The PDF includes the receipt image.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does SeeSight work on phones?

Yes, in the phone browser. There's no app to install.

What does SeeSight read from a receipt?

The merchant, the date and the amount, which become the expense record the traveler checks.

What are the possible decisions?

Approve, reject, approve part of the claim, or ask for a document, always with one comment.

How is the traveler told?

By email, as soon as the accountant decides.
